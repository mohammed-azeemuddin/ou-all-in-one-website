Privacy Policy

OU All In One app (Google Play: "OU Notes,Papers,Lab,Placements", package com.azeem.ou_app2) · Last updated: 15 November 2026

OU All In One ("the app") is a free study app for Osmania University students, made by an independent developer (TR Studios). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up or login and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers, and we do not use an advertising ID.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, recently opened papers, quiz scores, calculator inputs, theme choice and the date you last opened the app are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Papers you download are saved to the folder you choose on your phone.

How content is loaded

The list of papers and notes is read from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), and files are downloaded over encrypted connections (HTTPS) from Google Drive or our file server. Like any internet request, these services receive your IP address and basic technical information needed to deliver the content; we do not use it to identify you. Firebase App Check may be used to confirm that requests come from the genuine app. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Sponsored content

The app may in future show sponsored cards from local institutes or businesses. These are shown the same way to everyone and do not use your personal data.

The website (ouallinone.com)

This section is about the website only; the app does not include any of this. The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used, and Google AdSense to show ads that keep the site free. These Google services may set cookies or use similar technology, and receive your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you visit. Google may use this to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and block Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before these cookies are used. Your theme choice and placement prep progress on the website are saved only in your browser.

Links and email

Some items open web pages (for example YouTube or Telegram) in an in-app browser; those sites have their own privacy policies. If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send and use it only to reply.

Children

The app is meant for university students and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Copyright and removal

Question papers and notes are shared for study purposes. If you own content in the app and want it removed, long-press the file in the app and choose "Request removal", or email us. We act on valid requests promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
